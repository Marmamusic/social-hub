Privacy Policy — Martin Social Hub

Last updated: November 2026

Martin Social Hub is a private, single-owner publishing utility used solely by Martin Schmalzried to schedule posts to his own social media channels via official platform APIs.

  • No third-party user data is collected, stored, sold, or shared.
  • OAuth access tokens are stored exclusively in encrypted GitHub Actions Repository Secrets and used solely to publish authorized posts to the owner's accounts.
